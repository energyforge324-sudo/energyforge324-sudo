EnergyForge is an Indian manufacturing and engineering company working across upstream oil & gas,
well testing, production facilities and the wider process industries. We bring engineered equipment,
controlled manufacturing, field execution and lifecycle technical services together:
**one accountable partner from datasheet to start-up.**

## What we build

| Well testing & production | Process & storage |
|---|---|
| Three-phase & test separators | Surge & storage tanks (incl. KODs) |
| Indirect water-bath heaters | Vertical flare stacks |
| Sand traps & catchers | HP/LP piping & skids |
| Choke & flow manifolds | |

## Capabilities

- Skid-mounted process equipment: design and fabrication
- Pressure vessels, piping and structures
- Controls, instrumentation interfaces and documentation
- Brownfield execution and field support
- Lifecycle services: maintenance, refurbishment and spares
- Project-specific quality assurance
